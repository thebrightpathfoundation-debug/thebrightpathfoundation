Community Impact

Technology Meets Humanitarian Need

BrightPath Foundation has already begun its work on the ground. Our first program was delivered at an IDP (Internally Displaced Persons) camp, teaching young women how to create reusable sanitary pads — combining practical skills training with dignity, sustainability, and economic empowerment.

This is BrightPath in action: meeting communities where they are, with programs that create immediate, tangible impact.
