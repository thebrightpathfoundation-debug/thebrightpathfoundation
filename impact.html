Featured Story · 2025

Reusable Pads,
Lasting Dignity

BrightPath Foundation's first program was delivered at an Internally Displaced Persons (IDP) camp — reaching young women in one of Nigeria's most vulnerable communities.

Participants learned how to create reusable sanitary pads from locally available materials — a program that combined practical skills training with economic empowerment, hygiene education, and environmental sustainability.

Every woman who completed the program left not just with a skill, but with the ability to produce pads for themselves and their community — and the potential to generate income from their work.

This is what BrightPath looks like in the real world: meeting communities exactly where they are, with programs designed for their specific realities.

Our Journey

Foundation Milestones

May 2025

Official Registration — Federal Republic of Nigeria

BrightPath Foundation for Digital Learning is officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission. CAC Reg. No. 8453337 issued. Founding trustees: Moyosore Ejiroghene Jacob & Faith Eleke Ida.

2025

First Program Delivered — IDP Camp

BrightPath delivers its inaugural program at an IDP camp, teaching young women to create reusable sanitary pads. This marks the foundation's first direct community impact.

2025

Brand & Digital Infrastructure Built

Foundation website, corporate sponsorship materials, and brand identity established — positioning BrightPath for national and international fundraising and partnerships.

2026 Target

Tech Education Bootcamps Launch

Flagship coding and digital literacy bootcamps scheduled to launch in Lagos — the first of many nationwide cohorts planned for 2026.

2027 Target

Multi-State Expansion

Programs scale beyond Lagos to additional states, with the first community tech hubs established in underserved regions across Nigeria.

2028 Vision

National Scale — All 36 States

BrightPath achieves nationwide presence with programs operating across all 36 Nigerian states, positioning as the leading tech education NGO in Nigeria.
