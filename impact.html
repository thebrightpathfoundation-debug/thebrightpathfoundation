Featured Story · June 2026

40 Students.
100% Completion.

On 27 June 2026, BrightPath Foundation delivered its inaugural tech education program — "Build Your First Website" — at ST&T Regency International School, GRA Ikeja, Lagos.

40 secondary school students — 17 girls and 23 boys — spent half a day learning to code using CodePen. A professional tech tutor led the coding demo while a videographer captured every moment.

By the end of the session, every single student had built and presented a functional webpage. 100% completion. Not one student left without a certificate and a skill they didn't have that morning.

When asked if they wanted BrightPath to come back — the answer was immediate and unanimous.

Our Journey

Foundation Milestones

May 2025

Official Registration — Federal Republic of Nigeria

BrightPath Foundation for Digital Learning is officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission. CAC Reg. No. 8453337 issued. Founding trustees: Moyosore Ejiroghene Jacob & Faith Eleke Ida.

2025 – 2026

Brand & Digital Infrastructure Built

Foundation website, corporate sponsorship materials, and brand identity established — positioning BrightPath for national and international fundraising and partnerships.

27 June 2026

Inaugural Program — "Build Your First Website"

BrightPath delivers its first tech education workshop at ST&T Regency International School, GRA Ikeja, Lagos. 40 secondary school students trained. 100% completion rate. Every student built and presented a functional webpage.

2027 Target

Multi-State Expansion

Programs scale beyond Lagos to additional states, with the first community tech hubs established in underserved regions across Nigeria.

2028 Vision

National Scale — All 36 States

BrightPath achieves nationwide presence with programs operating across all 36 Nigerian states, positioning as the leading tech education NGO in Nigeria.
