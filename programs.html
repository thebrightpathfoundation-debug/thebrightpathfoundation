Our Programs

Eight Pathways to a Digital Future

From coding bootcamps to IDP community programs, every BrightPath initiative is designed with one goal: creating real, lasting change in the lives of Nigeria's youth.

💻
Program 01
Foundation

Tech Education &
Digital Literacy

Our flagship program provides practical, market-ready technology education to underserved communities. Participants learn coding, software development, and digital skills that have immediate value in Nigeria's growing tech economy.

  • Coding fundamentals & software development
  • Digital literacy & internet skills
  • Practical, hands-on learning approach
  • Certificates of completion for all graduates

Program 02
Connections

Mentorship Programmes

We connect young Nigerians with established tech professionals — creating structured mentorship relationships that provide guidance, career support, and real-world industry insight from across Nigeria and the diaspora.

  • 1-on-1 and group mentorship formats
  • Local, national, and diaspora mentors
  • Career planning and industry exposure
  • Networking and professional development

Program 03
Local Solutions

Community Innovation

Graduates apply their skills to real problems in their communities — building health tech platforms, agricultural tools, e-learning apps, and environmental solutions that create tangible local impact.

  • Participant-led technology projects
  • Health, agriculture, and education focus areas
  • Supported by foundation mentors and resources
  • Community problem-solving methodology

Program 04
Access

STEM Scholarships

Financial barriers should never prevent talent from flourishing. Our scholarship and bursary programme removes the cost barrier to quality STEM and technology education for deserving young Nigerians.

  • Full and partial scholarship awards
  • Collaboration with schools and institutions
  • Curriculum integration support
  • Annual scholarship application rounds

Program 05
Entrepreneurship

Incubation Hubs

We don't just train — we launch. BrightPath's incubation hubs provide the infrastructure, mentorship, and resources for youth to access the gig economy, secure remote employment, or build their own technology startups.

  • Physical and virtual hub spaces
  • Business development support
  • Gig economy and remote work onboarding
  • Seed funding access and investor connections

Equity

Gender Inclusion & Advocacy

Women and girls face unique barriers to technology education. Our targeted programme ensures equitable access, creates safe learning environments, and actively advocates for systemic change across Nigeria.

  • Women-focused training cohorts
  • Female mentorship and role model programmes
  • Policy advocacy for gender equity in tech
  • Safe, supportive learning environments

Program in Action · June 2026

Our First Program:
Build Your First Website

On 27 June 2026, BrightPath Foundation delivered its inaugural tech education workshop at ST&T Regency International School, GRA Ikeja, Lagos — 40 secondary school students, half a day, and a 100% completion rate.

Every student built and presented a functional webpage before leaving — proof that Nigeria's youth are ready. They just need the opportunity.

Communities We Reach
