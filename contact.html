Partnerships

How We Collaborate

🏢

Technology Companies

Tech companies can partner for curriculum co-development, equipment donations, employee volunteering, and talent pipeline access.

🎓

Universities & Institutions

Academic institutions can partner to integrate BrightPath curricula, provide campus space, and offer research collaboration.

🌐

International NGOs

Global development organisations can co-deliver programmes, share expertise, and amplify reach across underserved Nigerian communities.

🏛️

Government Bodies

Federal and state government agencies can partner on national digital skills initiatives and infrastructure development.

🌍

Diaspora Networks

Nigerian diaspora professionals and networks can contribute mentorship, funding, and international exposure to our participants.

📰

Media & Press

Media partners help us tell Nigeria's digital story — amplifying our impact and inspiring broader community engagement.
